Our Courses

A1 to B2 German training focused on Grammar, Speaking and Exam Skills for Goethe & telc certifications.

A1 Beginner

Goal: Introduce yourself, handle everyday situations, and understand simple German sentences.

  • Alphabet, pronunciation & numbers
  • Articles (der, die, das), verbs & sentence order
  • Greetings, family, shopping, time
  • Writing short forms, notes & messages
Duration: approx. 8–10 weeks
Prepares for: Goethe-Zertifikat A1 / telc Deutsch A1

A2 Elementary

Goal: Talk about routines, past events and immediate needs; read and respond to short everyday texts.

  • Perfekt & modal verbs in past and present
  • Dative, two-way prepositions & adjectives
  • Travel, health, work & housing situations
  • Formal/informal letters and short emails
Duration: approx. 8–10 weeks
Prepares for: Goethe-Zertifikat A2 / telc Deutsch A2
Enquire for A2 Batch →
B1 Intermediate

Goal: Work and study independently in German; express opinions, hopes and plans fluently.

  • Subordinate clauses & Konjunktiv II (wishes, polite requests)
  • Passive voice, relative clauses & reflexive verbs
  • Discussions, structured presentations & joint planning
  • Formal complaints, opinion essays & blog responses
Duration: approx. 10–12 weeks
Prepares for: Goethe-Zertifikat B1 / telc Deutsch B1
Enquire for B1 Batch →
B2 Upper Intermediate

Goal: Communicate fluently on complex technical and academic topics — ideal for jobs, nursing, and university.

  • Advanced grammar, multi-part connectors & participle clauses
  • Critical argumentation, debate & spontaneous discussions
  • Professional, medical and academic vocabulary
  • Detailed essays, reports & official correspondence
Duration: approx. 12–14 weeks
Prepares for: Goethe-Zertifikat B2 / telc Deutsch B2

How We Teach

Batch Format & Exam Skills

Our proven gurukul learning environment ensures every student thrives.

Small Batches

Maximum 10-15 students per batch for personal attention, individual speaking turns, and daily instructor feedback.

Regular Mock Tests

Full Goethe-Institut and telc pattern test series covering Lesen, Hören, Schreiben, and Sprechen with precise scoring.

Speaking Every Class

Interactive pair work, realistic German dialogues, and live role-plays in every single session to overcome speaking hesitation.
