About Us

A gurukul of knowledge, a confluence of languages.

ज्ञानस्य गुरुकुलम्, भाषायाः संगमः

"The Gurukul of Knowledge, the Confluence of Languages"

BEETHOVENS was founded in 2026 in Kishanpura, Rohtak, to bring world-class German language education to Haryana. We combine the personal mentoring, discipline and cultural warmth of an authentic Indian Gurukul with modern communicative European language pedagogy. With small batches of just 10-15 students and intensive Goethe & telc exam coaching, we empower our students to study, train, and build prosperous professional careers in Germany.
