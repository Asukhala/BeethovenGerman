Student Resources

Official Goethe-Institut and telc exam formats, scoring criteria, and sample practice tests for A1–B2.

Exam Format

The Four Modules

Goethe-Zertifikat and telc exams from A1 to B2 assess language proficiency across four core communication skills.

Lesen (Reading)

Understand authentic notices, classified ads, emails, announcements and short magazine articles.

Hören (Listening)

Comprehend loudspeaker announcements, telephone messages, radio clips, and everyday conversations.

Schreiben (Writing)

Fill out official registration forms, compose short emails, response letters, and structured opinion posts.

Sprechen (Speaking)

Introduce yourself, exchange information, ask questions, agree on plans, and present topics to partner & examiner.

Scoring & Passing Criteria

  • Goethe-Zertifikat: Each module is scored out of 100 points. A minimum of 60 points (60%) is required to pass each module.
  • Modular Flexibility: At B1 and B2, Goethe modules can be booked, taken, and passed individually.
  • telc Deutsch: Written and oral components are evaluated separately; approximately 60% is required in each to pass.
  • Official Results: Digital and physical certificates usually arrive within 2–4 weeks after the exam date.

Exam-Day Checklist

  • Carry your original valid passport / official photo ID (mandatory for entry).
  • Arrive at the testing centre at least 30 to 45 minutes prior to the scheduled exam time.
  • Bring approved blue or black ballpoint pens; electronic watches, phones and smart devices are prohibited in test rooms.
  • Carefully transfer all answers to the official answer sheet (Antwortbogen) before the allotted time ends.

Gurukul Method

5 Golden Rules to Master German

  1. Learn nouns always with their grammatical gender: Always memorize new vocabulary as a trio: article + noun + plural form (e.g. das Buch, die Bücher).
  2. Listen to spoken German for 20 minutes daily: Tune into Deutsche Welle (DW Learn German), Slow German podcasts, or YouTube language videos every morning.
  3. Write one short paragraph every day: Describe your morning routine, a memory, or an opinion, and get it reviewed by your Beethoven trainer.
  4. Pair speaking practice: Practice the oral role-plays with fellow students using official Goethe exam question cards.
  5. Take timed mock tests under exam conditions: Practice transferring your answers directly to the answer sheet within the strict time limits.
